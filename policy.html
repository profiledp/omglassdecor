Policies & Guidelines

Please read our terms before placing an order.

💳 1. Advance Payment Policy

To confirm your order and start the fabrication process, a 50% advance payment is mandatory.

  • Advance payment can be made via UPI, Bank Transfer, or Cash.
  • Work will begin only after the advance is received and acknowledged by our team.
  • The remaining 50% balance must be paid before or at the time of installation/delivery.

❌ 2. Cancellation Policy

We understand situations may change, but since glass is a customized product, the following rules apply:

  • Before Fabrication Starts: Free cancellation. 100% advance refund will be processed within 3-5 working days.
  • After Cutting/Fabrication: Orders cannot be cancelled once the glass has been cut to your size. The advance amount will be forfeited.

🔄 3. Return & Replacement Policy

Glass is a fragile and custom-made product. We DO NOT accept returns for "change of mind" or "wrong size ordered by customer".

  • Damaged in Transit: If the glass is broken during delivery by us, we will replace it for FREE. (Report within 24 hours with photos/video).
  • Manufacturing Defect: Any scratches or bubbles visible at the time of delivery will be replaced immediately.
  • After Installation: Once the glass is installed and approved by the customer, no returns or refunds will be entertained.
⚠️ Note: Always inspect the glass before installation. Once installed, the item is considered accepted as-is.

🛠️ 4. Warranty & Maintenance

We provide a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects (like seal failure in double glazing). This does not cover physical damage, scratches after installation, or improper cleaning.
