About Us

38+ Years of Excellence

Established in 1986, Om Glass Decor has been a pioneer in the glass industry in Udaipur. What started as a small glass shop has now grown into one of the most trusted names for premium glass and mirror solutions in the city.

We take pride in our craftsmanship, our attention to detail, and our commitment to delivering the highest quality products to our customers. Whether it's a simple bathroom mirror or a large-scale architectural glass installation, we treat every project with the same passion and precision.

Our Mission

To enhance the beauty and functionality of every space with premium, safe, and durable glass solutions. We aim to provide our customers with the best-in-class service, from consultation to installation.
